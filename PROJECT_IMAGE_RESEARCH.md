1. **"Brit Awards — Golden Arrow Winner" is a duplicate.**
   The award is a **British Arrows _Craft Gold Arrow_** (advertising awards), not the BRIT music
   awards. The winning film is **"Live Again" by The Chemical Brothers** — which is already in
   `data.json` as **"Chemical Brothers Live Again"** (it already has 6 `ChemBros/` images and a
   YouTube link). **Recommendation:** delete the new "Brit Awards" entry and instead enrich the
   existing Chemical Brothers entry with the award credit and category (see below). _No data
   change has been made yet — awaiting your call._
   Source: <https://www.britisharrows.com/films/british-arrows/2024/live-again-the-chemical-brothers>

2. **There are two "ARRI & Friends" entries** (Aug 2023 and Dec 2021) with identical descriptions.
   The Aug 2023 one was given the 2 `ArriFriends/` images earlier. Consider merging or
   differentiating their titles.

3. **"Scottish Whiskey Exhibit VR"** — the public-facing piece is an **immersive projection**
   installation (not headset VR). Title/tech wording may want a tweak for accuracy.

4. **"Framestore Polestar VP shoot"** — the well-documented public Polestar virtual-production
   demo was made by **NantStudios / Impossible Objects**, *not* Framestore. If your Polestar job
   was a separate Framestore shoot, the public imagery below won't represent it — please confirm.
